Explainable AI Financial Fraud Detection Engine

Scores payment transactions, picks the decision threshold that minimises dollar loss, and turns SHAP log-odds into compliance-ready reason codes.

Problem statement

1. Extreme imbalance

Fraud is about 1.8% of volume, so accuracy is meaningless. Precision-recall behaviour is what counts.

2. Asymmetric costs

A missed fraud costs a $250 chargeback; a false alarm costs $15 in review and customer friction.

3. Regulatory explainability

Adverse-action and model-risk rules require specific, defensible reasons for automated decisions.

System architecture

data_generator.py

25,000 chronologically ordered synthetic payments with a non-linear logistic risk model.

→

train_and_explain.py

Strict 80/20 chronological split, XGBoost with scale_pos_weight, cost-optimal threshold sweep.

→

reason_codes.py

TreeExplainer SHAP plots and top-3 audit reason codes per flagged transaction.

Leakage control: training uses df.iloc[:split_idx] and testing uses df.iloc[split_idx:], so the model never sees the future.

Cost-sensitive optimisation

t* = argmin over t in [0.05, 0.95] of ( C_FP × FP(t) + C_FN × FN(t) )
C_FP = $15.00  |  C_FN = $250.00

Because C_FN / C_FP ≈ 16.7, the optimum typically sits well below the default 0.50 cut-off.
